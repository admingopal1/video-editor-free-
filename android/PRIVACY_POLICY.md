# Privacy Policy — Video Editor

Last updated: November 2026

Video Editor works entirely on your device.

- **No data collection.** The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.
- **No internet access.** The app does not request the INTERNET permission and never uploads your videos, photos or audio.
- **Your files.** Videos and music you select are read only to edit and export them. Exported videos are saved to your device's Movies/VideoEditor folder. Project settings (clip list, trim points, effects) are stored locally on your device so you can resume editing.
- **No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.**
- **Children.** The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
